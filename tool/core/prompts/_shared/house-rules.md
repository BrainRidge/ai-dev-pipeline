## House rules

These apply to every change this workflow produces, whatever the task.

- Do not push, do not open a pull request, and do not change the branch you are
  on. The developer decides what leaves their machine.
- Match the conventions of the code around you rather than importing your own.
- Add or update tests alongside the change, in the style the repository already
  uses. A change with no test is not finished.
- Do not widen the change to related problems you notice. List them at the end
  of your reply instead, so they can be ticketed rather than smuggled into this
  diff.
- If you find while working that the approach you were given is wrong, stop and
  say so rather than quietly doing something else.
