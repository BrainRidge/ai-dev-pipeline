# 2. Goals and non-goals

> Part of the [AI Dev Workflow Phase 1 design](README.md).

## Goals

- Standardise the AI-assisted development process across all four platforms.
- Make workflow definitions editable without writing TypeScript.
- Centralise and version the prompts given to the AI.
- Produce an audit trail of what context went in and what artifact came out.
- Enable a new joiner to produce quality output by following the workflow.

## Non-goals for Phase 1

- Auditing the developer's conversation inside Copilot Chat (not technically possible under
  the chosen handoff model — see [Section 8](08-ai-handoff-step.md)).
- Any live integration with JIRA, Rovo or Confluence.
- Sharing task artifacts between developers.
- Archival or cleanup of old task folders.
- Auto-update of the extension.
